## Design Context

### Users
- Primary: B2B stakeholders in logistics and food/industrial operations.
- Context: Evaluate services/cases quickly, understand trust signals, and take a clear next step.
- Job to be done: Decide whether LAVI & CO can reduce operational friction and deliver measurable outcomes.

### Brand Personality
- Tone: Serious, precise, high-standard, consultative.
- Voice: Direct and outcome-oriented, not playful.
- Emotional goals: Confidence, control, clarity, and momentum.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Preferred feel: Dark, premium, structured B2B editorial.
- Visual constraints from brand: Official palette and logo geometry are fixed unless explicitly requested.
- Product intent: Keep visual identity distinctive while avoiding generic SaaS sameness.

### Design Principles
- Clarity over ornament: each block must communicate one business idea.
- Trust through consistency: stable hierarchy, consistent interaction patterns, predictable navigation.
- Actionability first: primary CTA should remain obvious across breakpoints.
- Measurable storytelling: claims should be anchored in concrete metrics/case evidence.
- Controlled motion: animation should orient and support, never distract.
